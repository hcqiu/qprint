## Usage boundaries

| Scenario | Commercial authorization required? |
| --- | --- |
| Personal learning, notes, and unpaid personal projects without a commercial purpose | No |
| Ordinary teaching and coursework at nonprofit educational institutions | No; ordinary tuition and academic salaries alone do not change this |
| Independent noncommercial academic research at nonprofit institutions | No; public research grants and scholarships alone do not change this |
| An institution's private service for its eligible teaching/research participants | No |
| Internal company use, research, evaluation, or work performed on a company's behalf | Yes, even without redistribution or direct revenue |
| Business-commissioned research, research on a business's behalf, or commercial product research | Yes; an unrestricted donation without commercial deliverables or preferential commercialization rights alone is not enough to make independent research commercial |
| Paid consulting, commercial training, commercial SaaS, advertising-supported or business-promotional services | Yes |
| Public hosted Qprint services or APIs | Yes, even if access is free; eligible private institutional teaching/research services have the exception above |
| Commercial product integration, bundled sales, resale, or paid sublicensing | Yes |
| Free sharing of original or modified copies solely for eligible noncommercial use | No, provided licenses and attribution are retained, changes are identified, and no additional commercial rights are purportedly granted |

Individual status, an academic label, or free access does not by itself exempt a use. The actual purpose and LICENSE definitions govern.

## Activating a commercial license

No commercial contact email or application link is currently published. Commercial use requires a prior written order signed by the rights holder or an authorized licensor and the licensee, expressly incorporating the [commercial terms](../COMMERCIAL-LICENSE.md). Downloading, inquiring, or making an unsolicited payment is not authorization.

The order must specify parties, versions, uses, territory, user/deployment limits, term, fees and payment terms, notice method, governing law, and dispute forum. Internal use, SaaS, integration, and resale rights must be expressly selected; internal-use rights do not include resale. This repository sets no price and promises no support, updates, perpetual use, or exclusivity by default.

Publishing the commercial terms does not grant a commercial license to every recipient: a signed order is required. Have counsel review the terms for the actual transaction and jurisdiction before entering commercial agreements.
